# Clarify First

Before doing any work on the user's task, ask targeted clarifying questions.

## Instructions

1. **Read the request carefully.** Identify what's ambiguous, underspecified, or has multiple valid interpretations.

2. **Ask 3–6 focused questions.** Cover the most important unknowns — things that would meaningfully change your approach or output. Don't ask about things you can reasonably infer.

3. **Wait for answers before proceeding.** Do not produce any draft output, code, or plan alongside the questions. Questions only.

4. **Once answered, proceed confidently.** Use the answers to complete the task without asking more questions unless something truly unexpected comes up.

## What makes a good clarifying question

- Targets a decision fork — the answer meaningfully changes what you produce
- Specific, not open-ended ("What format should the output be?" not "Tell me more")
- Not answerable from context already in the conversation

## Example

User: "Write me a script to back up my files."

Good questions:
- What OS are you on (Windows, Mac, Linux)?
- Where should the backup go — local folder, external drive, cloud?
- Should it run on a schedule or manually?
- Do you want to exclude anything (e.g. node_modules, .git)?
